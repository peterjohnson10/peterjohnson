Biography

Scholar, practitioner and institutional bridge-builder

I work across boundaries that usually separate scholarship, policy, finance, diplomacy and emerging technology.

My work asks how institutions can remain democratic, legitimate and effective when technology changes the conditions of coordination.

I am a PhD candidate in Business, Knowledge and Innovation Management at the University of the Basque Country. My doctoral research compares the governance of decentralized autonomous organizations with financial cooperatives.

Before focusing on research and advisory work, I served as a U.S. Foreign Service Officer and worked in finance and development at S&P Global, Citi and the African Development Bank. Through Blue Shift Labs by Ayadee, I have advised international institutions on digital economies, AI governance, FinTech, public-sector innovation and strategic foresight across more than thirty countries.
