Research / Cooperative Governance
Research area

Cooperative Governance

What can long-lived democratic organizations teach digital institutions about participation, scale, solidarity and resilience?

Cooperatives are not nostalgic alternatives to modern organizations. They are living laboratories of institutional design.

Comparative perspective

Cases include SICOOB, Mondragon, Rabobank and SACCO systems, with attention to one-member-one-vote systems, federated governance, education, professional management and mutualized risk.
