Research / DAO Governance
Research area

DAO Governance

Do blockchain organizations decentralize authority—or repackage familiar institutional problems in new technical forms?

Code changes the instruments of governance, but not the need for legitimacy, participation and institutional memory.

Current cases

My doctoral research examines MakerDAO/Sky and Aave alongside financial cooperatives, comparing participation, delegation, concentration of authority, resilience and governance evolution.

Key themes

Token voting, delegation, smart contracts, off-chain coordination, governance capture, constitutional change and the interaction between formal rules and social institutions.
