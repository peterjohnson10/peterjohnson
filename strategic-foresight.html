Strategic Foresight

How can institutions prepare for futures that cannot be forecast through linear extrapolation?

Foresight is not prediction. It is disciplined preparation for plural futures.

My work uses scenarios, systems thinking and institutional imagination to help leaders examine technological, political and economic discontinuities.
