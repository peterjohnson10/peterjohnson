Research / AI Governance
Research area

AI Governance

How should societies allocate authority between humans and machines while preserving accountability, legitimacy and institutional capacity?

AI governance is not only a problem of model safety. It is a problem of institutional design.

Research questions

Who is authorized to deploy AI? Which decisions may be delegated? How should organizations classify risk, document judgment, preserve contestability and learn from failure?

Applied focus

This work draws on my advisory experience with international development institutions and explores how AI governance can be operationalized in procurement, program delivery, public administration and organizational strategy.
