Current research

What is the governance-risk premium? Cooperatives versus DAOs

Democratic governance at scale: a comparative institutional analysis of financial cooperatives and decentralized autonomous organizations.

Doctoral research

Why do cooperatives and DAOs pay for trust differently?

The project compares how two democratic organizational families distribute the cost of trust. Cooperatives incur recurring costs through member education, deliberation and participation infrastructure. DAOs appear to concentrate governance costs in lower participation, concentrated voting power and occasional severe failures.

The central proposition is not that one form is inherently superior, but that governance outcomes emerge from institutional architecture rather than technological substrate alone.

Anchor cases

SICOOB UniMais Mantiqueira and MakerDAO/Sky.

Extension cases

OP and POP Bank Groups, Rabobank, Mondragon finance, Aave, Compound and Uniswap.

Methods

Interviews, institutional documents, supervisory data, financial stability indicators, on-chain voting histories and governance-forum analysis.
